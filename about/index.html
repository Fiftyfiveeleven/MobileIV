About Optimized Performance & Wellness

A Jupiter-based mobile IV therapy team of licensed nurses who come to you, so you can feel like yourself again without leaving home.

Who we are

Optimized Performance & Wellness is a mobile IV therapy provider based in Jupiter, Florida. Licensed registered nurses, overseen by a medical director, bring IV hydration and vitamin drips to homes, offices and hotels across northern Palm Beach County and the Treasure Coast. Every visit starts with a health screening.

Our approach

Our experienced healthcare team works with you to help you reach your best. Every drip on our menu is a starting point. We customize it to you and your situation, to optimize how you feel today so you can meet your goals and be your best self.

We started with a simple idea: when you feel awful, the last thing you should have to do is drive to a clinic and sit in a waiting room. So we come to you.

Your care team

Barry, RN

Every IV is overseen by Barry, our licensed registered nurse, who brings 5 years of emergency room experience to every visit.

Medical director

Our licensed nurses are overseen by a medical director.

Primary care telehealth

Your visit can be upgraded to a telehealth call with our primary care provider. Telehealth visits are available to schedule for a fee.

Screening first, always

Every visit starts with a health screening to make sure your drip is safe and right for you. Our safety process.

What we believe

  • Real people, fast replies. When you text, a real person (one of our RNs or healthcare professionals) gets back to you.
  • Honest about what IVs do. IV hydration supports recovery. It doesn't cure illness, and it isn't emergency care.
  • Tailored, not upsold. Often IV fluids plus the right medication is all it takes. No package needed.
