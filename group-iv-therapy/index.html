Group + event IV therapy

Bridal parties, family visits, sick households and team recovery days. We treat everyone in one visit, right where you are.

Text us your group size
Quick answer

Optimized Performance & Wellness can give IV drips to a whole household or group in one visit at your home, rental or hotel in Jupiter and Palm Beach County. Everyone 10 and older can be treated, each person gets their own health screening, and each can choose their own drip. Text us how many people so your nurse brings enough for everyone.

Popular reasons to book a group

  • Weddings + bridal parties: Glow drips before the big day, at your rental or hotel.
  • The whole house is sick: one visit instead of multiple urgent care trips. See Get Right.
  • Visiting family + friends: Travel Defense on arrival or before everyone flies home.
  • Teams + training groups: Recover after a race, tournament or hard training day.

How group visits work

  1. Text us your date, location, number of people and the drips you're interested in.
  2. We confirm timing and make sure your nurse brings enough for everyone.
  3. Each person is screened, then drips run at the same time, so the whole group is usually done in about an hour.

Group IV FAQ

Can you treat several people in one visit?

Yes, we treat the whole household or group in one visit, ages 10 and older. Let us know how many people when you text so your nurse brings enough for everyone.

Which drip is best before a wedding?

Glow is a favorite before weddings, photo shoots and galas. It has boosted glutathione and vitamin C plus biotin. Many guests also choose Foundational Meyers or Recover.

Can everyone pick a different drip?

Yes. Each person can choose their own drip, and every person gets their own health screening first.
