How Much Does Mobile IV Therapy Cost in Palm Beach County?

Every drip on our menu, what's in it, and what affects the price.

By the Optimized Performance & Wellness nursing team · Updated November 2026

Quick answer

At Optimized Performance & Wellness, mobile IV therapy costs $150 to $435 per drip, delivered to your home by a licensed nurse. Your Call (build your own) starts at $150, most vitamin drips are $235 to $350, Get Right is $435, and NAD+ can be added to any drip for $50.

What's included in the price

  • A licensed nurse who comes to you at your home, office, hotel or rental
  • A health screening before every single drip
  • 1 liter of IV fluids (Lactated Ringer's or normal saline) in every bag
  • The vitamins, minerals and nutrients listed for your drip
  • Setup and cleanup. You stay on the couch for 45 to 60 minutes.

What changes the cost

The drip you choose. Drips with boosted or optimized doses of ingredients like vitamin C and glutathione cost more than our everyday drips.

Add-ins. NAD+ is a $50 add-on to any drip. Your Call includes three add-ins of your choice, and many other options are available.

Optional medications. In the Get Right drip, medications for pain, nausea, upset stomach or allergic reactions are available when appropriate after your nurse's assessment.

Is mobile IV therapy worth it compared to a clinic?

With mobile IV therapy you don't drive while you're sick, sit in a room with other sick people, or wait your turn. You can also treat the whole household in one visit. For many people, that convenience is the main reason they choose an at-home IV.
