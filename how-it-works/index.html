Three steps. Zero waiting rooms. Here's exactly what happens from your first text to the last drop.

Booking + visit FAQ

How do I book?

Text us at +1 (561) 503-0046 or +1 (561) 312-6570 with the drip you want (or how you're feeling) and your address. A real person replies and locks in the soonest time.

How long does a drip take?

About 45 to 60 minutes from setup to finish.

What should I do before my nurse arrives?

Drink some water if you can, have a comfortable place to sit or lie down, and have a list of your medications and allergies handy. Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily.

Does it hurt?

Just a quick pinch when the IV goes in. After that most people barely notice it.

Can you treat the whole family?

Yes, we treat ages 10 and older. Let us know how many people when you text so your nurse brings enough for everyone.

Text us how you're feeling and where you are. We'll handle the rest.
