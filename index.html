What is mobile IV therapy?

The IV comes to your couch.

Quick answer

Mobile IV therapy is IV hydration and vitamin infusions given by a licensed nurse at your home, office or hotel. Optimized Performance & Wellness serves Jupiter, Palm Beach County and the Treasure Coast. Each visit starts with a health screening, uses 1 liter of IV fluids plus vitamins chosen for your goal, and takes 45 to 60 minutes. Drips start at $150.

Feeling awful is hard enough. Instead of driving to a clinic and waiting with other sick people, you text us, a nurse arrives, and you rest while the drip does its work. Our healthcare team customizes every drip to you and your situation, so you feel your best today and can keep going after your goals.

Still wondering? Text or call +1 (561) 503-0046 or +1 (561) 312-6570, or email optiperfwell@gmail.com, and a real person, one of our RNs or healthcare professionals, will get back to you.

Does an IV actually help when I'm sick?

When you're sick it's easy to get dehydrated, especially with fever, vomiting or not eating. IV fluids rehydrate you faster than sipping water, and many people feel noticeably better after. It supports your recovery, it doesn't cure the illness.

How long does a drip take?

About 45 to 60 minutes from setup to finish.

Can you treat the whole family?

Yes, we treat ages 10 and older. Let us know how many people when you text so your nurse brings enough for everyone.

Who gives the IV?

Every IV is overseen by Barry, our licensed registered nurse. Every visit starts with a health screening to make sure your drip is safe and right for you.

Does it hurt?

Just a quick pinch when the IV goes in. After that most people barely notice it.
